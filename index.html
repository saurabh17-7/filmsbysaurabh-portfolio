About

Not just what it looked like. What it felt like.

I'm Saurabh Pawar, a visual storyteller working across social content, cinematography, travel films and photography.

I look for the detail that makes a story feel real: the atmosphere of a place, the energy behind a brand, the split second that turns a moment into a memory.

From outdoor adventures to brand-led visuals, my approach is simple. Keep the story honest, make every frame intentional, and make work that stays with you after the screen goes dark.
